## EMAIL 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** Why Korean and Mexican food fit together
**Preview text:** Both cuisines run on chili, fermentation and slow heat.

Hi,

People call Korean-Mexican food a gimmick. It isn't. Both kitchens are built on the same three things:

1. **Chili as a foundation, not a garnish.** Korea has gochugaru and gochujang; Mexico has guajillo, ancho and chipotle.
2. **Fermentation.** Kimchi and doenjang on one side; masa made from nixtamalized corn and pickled chiles on the other.
3. **Slow heat.** Braised short ribs and barbacoa are the same idea in two languages.

So a kimchi salsa on a birria taco isn't a stunt. It's two cuisines doing the same job.

If you want to cook this way at home, you need surprisingly few ingredients. I put them in one list: **[the Korean-Mexican pantry starter kit](https://borderlesskitchenseries.com/journal/korean-mexican-pantry-starter-kit)**.

— Sebastian

## EMAIL 3 — Day 4
**Subject:** The three jars that do most of the work
**Preview text:** Gochujang, gochugaru, and one bag of dried chiles.

Hi,

If you buy only three things for Korean-Mexican cooking, buy these:

**1. Gochujang.** Buy the thick paste in a tub, not the squeeze-bottle "gochujang sauce", and start at medium heat. Here's how to choose: **[best gochujang brands](https://borderlesskitchenseries.com/journal/best-gochujang-brands-how-to-choose)**. Ran out? **[These substitutes work](https://borderlesskitchenseries.com/journal/gochujang-substitute-what-to-use-instead)**.

**2. Gochugaru.** Korean red pepper flakes. Nothing else tastes quite like them. It's the paste vs flakes question: **[gochujang vs gochugaru](https://borderlesskitchenseries.com/journal/gochujang-vs-gochugaru-difference)**.

**3. Dried guajillo and ancho chiles.** Together they make most Mexican red sauces: **[ancho vs guajillo vs pasilla](https://borderlesskitchenseries.com/journal/ancho-vs-guajillo-vs-pasilla-dried-chiles)**.

With those three, everything in Email 1 is within reach.

— Sebastian

P.S. If you make tortillas, a cast-iron press changes everything: **[how to choose a tortilla press](https://borderlesskitchenseries.com/journal/best-tortilla-press-how-to-choose)**.
