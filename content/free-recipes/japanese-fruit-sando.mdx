The fruit sando requires one piece of planning: **position the fruit along the diagonal cut line before sealing**. The visual cross-section is the entire presentation — you need to know where to cut before you assemble.

## What You Need

- 4 thick slices Japanese shokupan (milk bread), crusts removed
- 240ml heavy cream, very cold
- 2 tbsp sugar
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- 4 large strawberries, hulled
- 1 kiwi, peeled and sliced into rounds
- Optional: seedless green grapes, mandarin segments

## How to Make It

**Whip the cream:** Chill the bowl and beaters for 10 minutes in the freezer. Whip cream to soft peaks, add sugar and vanilla, whip to stiff peaks. The cream should hold its shape completely when the beater is lifted.

**Plan the fruit:** Before assembling, decide exactly where your diagonal cut will go. The fruit you want to appear in the cross-section must sit directly under that cut line. For this recipe: position one strawberry (point down) and two kiwi rounds along the diagonal.

**Assemble:** Spread a thick, even layer of cream (about 1.5cm) over all four bread slices. Place fruit in planned positions on two slices, pressing gently into the cream. Cover each fruit-topped slice with a second bread slice. Press gently.

**Chill:** Wrap each sandwich tightly in plastic wrap. Refrigerate 30 minutes minimum. The chill time sets the cream around the fruit and makes the cut clean.

**Cut:** Remove from plastic. Using a sharp knife in one clean sawing motion (don't press down — it crushes the bread), cut diagonally along the line you planned. The fruit cross-sections appear.

Serve immediately, cut-side facing up.
