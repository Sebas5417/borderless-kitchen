The rice is seasoned with sesame oil, not vinegar. That one difference separates gimbap from Japanese maki completely.

## What You Need

- 3 cups short-grain rice, cooked
- 4 full sheets nori
- 1.5 tbsp sesame oil + extra for brushing
- ½ tsp salt

**Fillings:**
- Danmuji (yellow pickled radish), cut in long strips — essential
- 2 cups spinach, blanched and squeezed dry, seasoned with sesame oil + salt
- 2 eggs, cooked into a flat omelet and cut in strips
- 2 carrots, julienned and stir-fried with salt
- 4 strips surimi (imitation crab) or ham

## How to Make It

**Season rice:** While hot, fold in sesame oil and salt. Cool to slightly warm.

**Assemble each roll:** Nori shiny-side down on bamboo mat. Spread ¾ cup rice evenly, leaving 3cm gap at far edge. Lay fillings in a horizontal line across the bottom third.

**Roll:** Lift the bottom edge and roll toward the far edge, pressing firmly as you go. Seal the edge.

**Brush and slice:** Brush exterior with sesame oil. Cut into 2cm rounds with a sharp knife, wiping clean between cuts.

Serve at room temperature. No soy sauce needed — already seasoned.
