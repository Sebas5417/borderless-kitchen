Salt the cucumber first to draw out excess water. This prevents the dressing from becoming diluted.

## What You Need

- 2 Japanese cucumbers or 1 English cucumber
- ½ tsp salt

**Dressing:**
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- ½ tsp sesame oil

**Optional add-ins:** wakame seaweed (soaked and drained), cooked shrimp, octopus, crab

## How to Make It

**Salt cucumbers:** Slice cucumbers very thin — a mandoline if you have one. Toss with salt. Let sit 10 minutes. Squeeze firmly to remove water.

**Make dressing:** Combine rice vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, and sesame oil. Stir until sugar dissolves.

**Dress:** Toss salted, squeezed cucumber with dressing. Add wakame or seafood if using.

**Rest:** Let sit 10 minutes in the refrigerator for the flavors to develop.

Serve cold, topped with sesame seeds.
