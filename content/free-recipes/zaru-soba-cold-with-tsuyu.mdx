Soba loses texture fast. Serve immediately after rinsing. Do not let it sit.

## What You Need

- 200g dried soba noodles

**Tsuyu dipping sauce:**
- 240ml dashi
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin (briefly heated and cooled to burn off alcohol)

**Garnish:** sliced scallion, wasabi, toasted nori strips

## How to Make It

**Make tsuyu:** Combine dashi, soy sauce, and cooled mirin. Chill completely. (Make ahead — keeps refrigerated 5 days.)

**Cook soba:** Bring 2L water to a vigorous boil. No salt. Add soba. Cook 5-6 minutes, testing frequently. Drain.

**Rinse:** Rinse under cold running water, rubbing gently with your hands to remove surface starch. Shake off excess water.

**Serve:** Arrange noodles on bamboo or a plate. Pour tsuyu into individual small cups.

**Eating:** Dip the bottom third of each chopstick-bundle into tsuyu. Eat. Add scallion and wasabi to the tsuyu (not the noodles).

**Sobayu:** Add the starchy cooking water to remaining tsuyu at the end. Drink as broth.
