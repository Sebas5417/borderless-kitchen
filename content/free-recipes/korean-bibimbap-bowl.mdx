Every component is cooked and seasoned separately. This is not shortcut cooking — it is the correct method. Mix only at the table.

## What You Need

**Rice:** 2 bowls cooked short-grain rice

**Vegetables (each seasoned separately):**
- 2 cups spinach, blanched, squeezed dry, tossed with sesame oil + salt
- 1 carrot, julienned, stir-fried with garlic + sesame oil
- 1 cup bean sprouts, blanched, seasoned with sesame oil + salt
- 1 zucchini, julienned, salted, squeezed dry, stir-fried
- 4 shiitake mushrooms, sliced, stir-fried with soy sauce + sesame oil

**Beef:**
- 150g ground beef or thinly sliced ribeye
- 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tsp sesame oil + 1 tsp sugar + ½ tsp gochugaru

**Per bowl:** 1 fried egg, sunny-side up

**Sauce (bibimbap gochujang):**
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp water (to loosen)
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## How to Make It

**Cook all vegetables separately** — each takes 2-3 minutes in a hot pan with a little oil.

**Cook beef:** Stir-fry marinated beef 3-4 minutes over high heat.

**Assemble:** Place rice in bowl. Arrange each vegetable in a section around the bowl. Place beef. Set fried egg in the center. Add 1-2 tbsp bibimbap sauce.

**Serve.** Let the person mix their own bowl at the table.

*For dolsot bibimbap (stone pot version): heat the stone bowl with oil until smoking, add rice, press down, add toppings, and serve immediately. The crust forms at the bottom while eating.*
