The tofu must be sundubu — the very soft, barely-set style. Regular soft tofu is not the same.

## What You Need

- 1 tube sundubu (450g uncurdled soft tofu)
- 150g shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small zucchini, sliced
- 2 eggs
- 1.5 cups anchovy broth
- 3 scallions, sliced

**Seasoning:**
- 2 tbsp gochugaru
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced

## How to Make It

**Build the base:** In a stone pot or heavy saucepan, heat sesame oil over medium-high. Add garlic. Cook 30 seconds. Add gochugaru and gochujang. Stir 1 minute to bloom the spice.

**Add broth + vegetables:** Pour in anchovy broth. Add soy sauce. Add zucchini and mushrooms. Simmer 4 minutes.

**Add shrimp:** Cook 2-3 minutes until pink.

**Add sundubu:** Squeeze the tube gently to release tofu in large irregular pieces. Simmer 3-4 minutes — do not stir vigorously.

**Egg:** Crack egg onto the surface. Cover 1 minute. Egg should still be slightly runny.

**Finish:** Scatter scallions. Serve immediately over rice.
