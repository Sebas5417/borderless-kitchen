The pork must be fully tender before slicing — a chopstick should go in with almost no resistance. Under-cooked bossam is tough and chewy.

## What You Need

- 800g pork belly, skin-on if possible

**Simmering aromatics:**
- 3 tbsp doenjang + 5 cloves garlic + 3 slices ginger + 3 scallions + 1 onion halved + 1 tsp black peppercorns + 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp sake

**For serving:**
- Napa cabbage or perilla leaves (ssam)
- Kkakdugi or baechu kimchi
- Saeujeot (fermented shrimp)
- Ssamjang (3 tbsp doenjang + 1 tbsp gochujang + 1 tsp sesame oil + garlic + scallion)

## How to Make It

**Blanch:** Cover pork with cold water, boil 5 minutes. Drain. Rinse. This removes impurities.

**Simmer:** Return pork to pot with fresh water + all aromatics. Bring to boil, reduce to steady simmer. Cook 1.5-2 hours until chopstick-tender.

**Rest 10-15 minutes** before slicing 5mm thin, against the grain.

**Assemble wraps:** Leaf + pork slice + kimchi piece + ssamjang + garlic + eat in one bite.
