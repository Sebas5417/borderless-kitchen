The most important thing about kimchi fried rice: **use old kimchi**. Kimchi that's been fermenting in your refrigerator for 4-8 weeks has developed the acid and depth that makes this dish work. Fresh kimchi produces a flat result.

## What You Need

- 2 cups day-old short-grain rice
- 1 cup aged kimchi, roughly chopped + 3 tbsp kimchi juice
- ½ can Spam (200g), cut into small cubes
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 eggs
- Sesame seeds and scallions to serve

## How to Make It

**Crisp the Spam:** Heat oil in a large cast iron skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add Spam cubes. Cook, stirring occasionally, 3-4 minutes until golden and caramelized on the edges. Remove and set aside.

**Build the base:** In the same pan, add garlic and cook 30 seconds. Add kimchi. Cook 3-4 minutes until it begins to caramelize and soften.

**Bloom the gochujang:** Add gochujang directly to the pan. Let it cook against the hot surface for 30 seconds, stirring. This removes the raw paste taste.

**Add rice:** Add cold day-old rice. Break up any clumps with a spatula. Stir-fry 2 minutes. Add soy sauce and kimchi juice. Return Spam to the pan. Toss everything to combine.

**Make the crispy bottom:** Spread rice in an even layer. Don't stir for 2-3 minutes. Let the bottom crust. You'll hear it sizzle louder when it's ready.

**Finish:** Drizzle sesame oil around the edges. Slide to plates.

**Fry the eggs:** In a small pan, fry eggs over medium heat. Keep the yolk runny.

**Serve:** Rice topped with fried eggs, sesame seeds, and scallions. Eat immediately.
