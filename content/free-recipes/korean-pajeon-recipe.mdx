More oil = crispier edges. This is not a health dish. Embrace it.

## What You Need

**Batter:**
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp rice flour
- ¾ cup cold water (ice cold)
- ½ tsp salt

**Per pancake:**
- 6-8 scallions, trimmed
- 3 tbsp neutral oil

**Dipping sauce:**
- 2 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp rice vinegar + ½ tsp sesame oil + pinch gochugaru

## How to Make It

**Mix batter:** Combine flour, rice flour, salt, and cold water. Mix until just combined — a few lumps are fine.

**Heat the oil:** Add 3 tbsp oil to a 25cm pan over medium-high. Oil is hot enough when a drop of batter sizzles immediately.

**Arrange scallions:** Lay scallions flat and parallel in the pan.

**Pour batter:** Pour half the batter over the scallions to bind and coat. Don't disturb.

**Cook 3-4 minutes** until edges are set and golden brown (lift the edge to check).

**Flip** with confidence in one quick motion. Cook 2-3 more minutes.

**Serve** immediately with dipping sauce.
