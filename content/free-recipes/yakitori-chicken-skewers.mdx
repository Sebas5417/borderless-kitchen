Baste 3-4 times per skewer. Each basting builds the lacquered crust. Less basting = lighter color = less complex flavor.

## What You Need

- 500g boneless chicken thighs (skin-on), cut into 2-3cm cubes
- 8 bamboo skewers (soaked in water 30 min)
- 2 scallions, cut into 3cm pieces (for negima skewers)

**Tare sauce:**
- 100ml soy sauce
- 100ml mirin
- 2 tbsp sake
- 2 tbsp sugar

## How to Make It

**Make tare:** Simmer all tare ingredients in a small pot 15-20 minutes until reduced by a third. Should coat a spoon. Cool.

**Thread skewers:** For momo (thigh): 4-5 chicken cubes per skewer, skin facing same direction. For negima: alternate chicken and scallion pieces.

**Grill on high heat:** Cast iron grill pan or outdoor grill. 2-3 minutes per side.

**Baste:** After first flip, brush with tare. Flip. Brush again. Flip. Brush one final time, cook 30 seconds. Total: 3-4 bastings.

**Shio (salt) version:** Skip tare. Season with fine sea salt. Finish with lemon juice.
