Do not skip frying the chunjang. Unfired chunjang tastes harsh and raw. Two minutes of frying in oil transforms it.

## What You Need

- 400g fresh jjajangmyeon noodles (or thick udon)
- 300g pork belly, small cubes
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 2 cups cabbage, chopped

**Sauce:**
- 4 tbsp chunjang (Korean black bean paste)
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 cups water
- 2 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp oyster sauce + 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp potato starch + 4 tbsp water (slurry)

**Garnish:** Julienned cucumber, danmuji

## How to Make It

**Fry chunjang:** Heat oil in wok. Add chunjang. Fry stirring 2-3 minutes until it darkens and smells mellow. Remove to bowl.

**Cook pork:** Render and brown pork belly cubes in same wok, 5-7 minutes.

**Add vegetables:** Add onion, zucchini, cabbage. Stir-fry 5 minutes.

**Add sauce:** Return fried chunjang + water + sugar + oyster sauce + soy sauce. Simmer 8-10 minutes.

**Thicken:** Stir in potato starch slurry until glossy and thick.

**Cook noodles:** Boil, drain, briefly rinse. Divide into bowls. Sauce over top.

**Mix vigorously** before eating.
