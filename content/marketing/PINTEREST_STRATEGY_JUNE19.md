## Pin Design Strategy

### Format
- **Vertical 2:3 ratio** (1000x1500px) — best distribution
- Portrait orientation only — landscape pins get suppressed

### Visual Style for Borderless Kitchen
- Dark moody backgrounds (dark wood, slate, black marble) — distinguishes from typical bright food photography
- White or gold text overlay
- Japanese + Italian ingredient pairings in the same frame
- Ceramic bowls, handmade pasta, rustic textures

### Text Overlay Formula
Every pin needs a readable headline. Format:
- "How to Make [DISH] with [JAPANESE TWIST]"
- "[DISH]: The Japanese Version"
- "Why [JAPANESE INGREDIENT] Makes [ITALIAN DISH] Better"
- "The [SCIENCE/SECRET] Behind [DISH]"

## Posting Schedule

**Start:** 3-5 pins per day (use Tailwind scheduler)
**Optimal times:** 8-11pm EST (when Pinterest traffic peaks)
**Re-pin ratio:** 80% your content, 20% others (build board content)
**Consistency:** daily for first 3 months, then 3-4x/week

**Monthly cadence:**
- Week 1: Recipe pins (highest traffic)
- Week 2: Technique pins
- Week 3: Science/educational pins
- Week 4: Book promo pins
