The foundational stock of Japanese cuisine. In its simplest form, *kombu* (kelp) is steeped in cold water, then *katsuobushi* is added briefly off the heat. The result is a clear, almost weightless liquid that carries umami without any of the body or fat of a Western stock.

It is the technical opposite of a French *fond*: nothing is browned, nothing is reduced, and the work is over in twenty minutes.
