The foundational stock of Japanese cuisine. In its simplest form, [kombu](/culture/kombu) is steeped in cold water, then [katsuobushi](/culture/bonito) is added briefly off the heat. The result is a clear, almost weightless liquid that carries [umami](/culture/umami) without any of the body or fat of a Western stock.

It is the technical opposite of a French *fond*: nothing is browned, nothing is reduced, and the work is over in twenty minutes.
