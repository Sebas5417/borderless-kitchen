Tahini is a paste of ground sesame seeds, usually hulled and lightly toasted. It's essential to hummus, baba ghanoush and tahini sauce, and it's used in Middle Eastern sweets like halva.

## Tahini vs Asian sesame paste

Chinese and Japanese sesame pastes are usually made from **deeply toasted** sesame seeds and taste darker and nuttier. Tahini is milder and paler. They can substitute for each other in a pinch, but the flavor differs.

## Using it

Good tahini is pourable and smooth. Stir well before using, because the oil separates. Whisked with lemon juice, garlic and water, it first seizes and then loosens into a creamy sauce. Store it in a cool, dark cupboard; refrigerating it makes it thick.
