The pasilla (*chile pasilla*, "little raisin") is the dried form of the chilaca pepper: long, thin and nearly black, with mild to medium heat and an earthy, slightly bitter, raisin-like flavor. It's one of the "holy trinity" of mole chiles with ancho and mulato.

## A naming warning

In some US stores, especially in California, fresh poblanos and dried anchos are labeled "pasilla." A true dried pasilla is long and slim, not wide and heart-shaped like an ancho. Check the shape before you buy.

## Using it

Prepare it like other dried chiles: stem, seed, toast briefly, soak and blend. Pasilla adds depth to moles, salsas and stews.
