Turmeric is the rhizome of a plant in the ginger family. Dried and ground, it's the bright yellow-orange spice behind curry powders, Indian dals and many Southeast Asian curry pastes. Fresh turmeric looks like a small, orange-fleshed ginger root.

Its flavor is earthy, a little bitter and peppery; its color is much stronger than its taste. A little goes a long way.

## Using it

Bloom ground turmeric briefly in hot oil or ghee at the start of cooking to soften its raw, dusty edge. It stains hands, cutting boards and clothes, so handle it with care.

Buy ground turmeric in small amounts and keep it airtight, away from light: it fades and loses aroma within months.
