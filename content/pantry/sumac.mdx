Sumac is a deep red spice made from the dried, ground berries of the sumac shrub. It's tart and lemony, used across Middle Eastern and Eastern Mediterranean cooking to add sourness without liquid.

It's essential in *za'atar* blends and *fattoush* salad, and it's sprinkled over kebabs, onions, rice, hummus and grilled vegetables.

## Buying

Culinary sumac comes from *Rhus coriaria* and is sold at spice shops and Middle Eastern grocers. It's unrelated to the poison sumac of North America. Good sumac is a vivid red-purple; brownish sumac is old.
