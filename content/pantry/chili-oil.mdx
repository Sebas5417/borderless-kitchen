Chili oil is oil infused with dried chili flakes, made by pouring hot oil over the chilies, often with spices like Sichuan peppercorn, star anise and ginger. It's a staple condiment of Sichuan cooking. **Chili crisp** is the chunkier version, packed with crunchy fried shallots, garlic and sometimes soybeans.

## Using it

Spoon it over dumplings, noodles, eggs, tofu or rice. Mixed with soy sauce and black vinegar, it's the classic dumpling dip. Outside Chinese cooking, a drizzle works on pizza, burrata or tacos.

## Buying

Look at the ingredient list: good chili oil is mostly oil, chilies and aromatics. Stir before using, because the flakes sink. Keep it in a cool, dark place, and refrigerate jars with fresh garlic or shallot once opened if the label says so.
