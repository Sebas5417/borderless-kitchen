Canned coconut milk is made by pressing grated coconut flesh with water. It's the base of Thai curries, many Indian and Malaysian dishes, and desserts across Southeast Asia. It's different from the thin coconut milk sold in cartons as a dairy alternative for drinking.

## Full-fat vs light

Full-fat coconut milk is richer and separates in the can into a thick cream on top and thinner milk below. In Thai curries, the cream is often fried with the curry paste first to release its fragrance. "Light" coconut milk is simply diluted.

## Buying

Check the ingredient list: the best cans are mostly coconut and water. Many brands add guar gum or other stabilizers, which keep the milk smooth but stop the cream from separating. Shake the can before using unless the recipe asks for the cream separately.
