Ponzu is a Japanese citrus sauce. Most bottles sold as ponzu are **ponzu shoyu**: soy sauce mixed with citrus juice (yuzu, sudachi or other sour citrus), rice vinegar, mirin and dashi. The result is salty, sour and savory at once, thinner and brighter than soy sauce alone.

## Using it

- Dipping sauce for hot pot (*shabu-shabu*), dumplings and grilled fish.
- Dressing for seared tataki, salads and cold tofu.
- A splash over butter-sautéed mushrooms or a steak.

Once opened, refrigerate it; the citrus flavor fades over a few months.
