Umeboshi are Japanese salt-pickled *ume*, a fruit related to the apricot and plum. They're intensely sour and salty, often colored red with red shiso leaves. One is the classic filling for onigiri and the red center of a *hinomaru bento*.

## Using it

- Tucked into onigiri or served with plain rice or rice porridge.
- Pitted and mashed into dressings and sauces for a sour-salty kick, much like you'd use preserved lemon or capers.
- *Bainiku* (umeboshi paste) is sold ready-made in tubes and jars.

Traditional umeboshi are very salty; lower-salt versions, sometimes sweetened with honey, are common too. Check the label for salt content if it matters.
