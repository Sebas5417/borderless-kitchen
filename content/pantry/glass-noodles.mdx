Glass noodles are made from starch rather than wheat, and they turn translucent and slippery when cooked. Korean **dangmyeon** are made from sweet potato starch; they're thicker and chewier than the mung bean glass noodles common in Chinese and Southeast Asian cooking.

Dangmyeon are the noodle in **japchae**, the stir-fried noodle dish with vegetables, beef and sesame oil, and they're added to Korean stews and hot pots.

## Cooking

Boil dangmyeon for about 6 to 8 minutes until chewy, then drain and rinse. Cut them a few times with kitchen scissors: they're very long. Toss with a little sesame oil so they don't stick.

Starch noodles are naturally wheat-free, but check the label if you need them gluten-free.
