Tamarind is a sour-sweet fruit that grows in brown pods. Its sticky pulp is the souring agent in pad thai, many Indian chutneys and curries, Southeast Asian soups and sauces, and Mexican *agua de tamarindo* and candies.

## Forms

- **Pulp block:** compressed pulp with seeds. Soak a piece in hot water, squeeze, and strain to make tamarind water. It has the best flavor.
- **Concentrate or paste:** ready to use, but brands vary a lot in strength and sometimes in sweetness, so add it gradually.

Tamarind keeps for months in a cool cupboard; refrigerate opened concentrate.
