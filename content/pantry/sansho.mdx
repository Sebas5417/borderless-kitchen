Sansho is the Japanese relative of the Sichuan peppercorn: the ground husks of a prickly ash. It has a bright, citrusy aroma and causes a tingling, slightly numbing sensation on the tongue.

It's the classic seasoning for grilled eel (*unagi*), and it's one of the spices in *shichimi togarashi*. The young leaves (*kinome*) are used as a fragrant garnish in spring.

## Using it

Sprinkle it on at the end: on grilled eel, yakitori, fatty meats, or a bowl of miso soup. Ground sansho loses its fragrance quickly, so buy small amounts and keep it sealed.
