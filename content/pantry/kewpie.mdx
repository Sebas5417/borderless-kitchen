Kewpie is Japan's best-known mayonnaise, sold in a soft squeeze bottle with a star-shaped tip. It's made with egg yolks only (no whites) and its own blend of vinegars, which makes it richer, tangier and more savory than typical American mayonnaise.

## Where it goes

- Japanese potato salad and egg sandwiches (*tamago sando*).
- Squeezed in thin lines over okonomiyaki and takoyaki.
- With chili or yuzu kosho as a quick dipping sauce for karaage.

The US-market version of Kewpie is made to a slightly different recipe from the Japanese one. Imported Japanese bottles are sold too; check the label if you want the original.
