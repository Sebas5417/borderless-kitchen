Yuzu kosho is a Japanese paste of yuzu citrus peel, fresh chili and salt, fermented briefly. Green yuzu kosho uses green chilies and unripe yuzu; red yuzu kosho uses red chilies and ripe yuzu. Despite the name, it contains no black pepper: *kosho* is a regional word for chili in Kyushu, where it comes from.

It's salty, hot and intensely aromatic. A small dab goes a long way.

## Using it

Dab it on grilled meat or fish, yakitori, hot pot and sashimi. Stir a little into mayonnaise, butter or a vinaigrette. Refrigerate once opened.
