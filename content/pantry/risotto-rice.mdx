Risotto rice means the Italian short- to medium-grain varieties grown for risotto, mainly **Arborio**, **Carnaroli** and **Vialone Nano**. They're plump, starchy grains that release starch into the hot stock as you stir, thickening it into a creamy sauce, while the center of each grain stays slightly firm.

- **Carnaroli** holds its shape best and is the most forgiving.
- **Arborio** is the easiest to find and very creamy, but softens sooner.
- **Vialone Nano** is a smaller Veneto rice favored for loose, flowing risottos.

Don't rinse risotto rice: the surface starch is what makes the sauce.
