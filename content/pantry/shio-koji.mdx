Koji is rice (or barley or soybeans) inoculated with the mold *Aspergillus oryzae*. It's the starter behind miso, soy sauce, sake and mirin. **Shio koji** is koji mixed with salt and water and left to ferment into a thick, sweet-salty paste.

Its enzymes break proteins and starches down into amino acids and sugars. In practice that means shio koji seasons, tenderizes and helps browning all at once.

## Using it

- **Marinade:** coat fish, chicken or pork with about 10% of its weight in shio koji and refrigerate for a few hours to overnight. Wipe off most of it before cooking: the sugars burn easily.
- **Seasoning:** use it in place of salt in dressings, soups and pickles, at roughly twice the amount of salt you'd use.

Shio koji is sold ready-made as a paste or liquid. Keep it refrigerated once opened.
