Jasmine and basmati are wrong for Japanese and Korean food. They are long-grain rices, bred to cook up separate and fluffy. Sushi, onigiri, bibimbap and kimbap need the opposite — grains that hold together without turning to paste. That comes from japonica rice: short- and medium-grain varieties with more amylopectin, the starch that makes cooked rice sticky.

The science is in our [Japanese rice guide](/journal/japanese-rice-science-guide). This page is about what to buy.

---

## Short-Grain vs Medium-Grain

| | Short-grain | Medium-grain (Calrose type) |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | Round, plump | Slightly longer |
| Texture | Sticky, chewy, glossy | Soft, slightly less sticky |
| Best for | Sushi, onigiri, premium table rice | Everyday rice, bibimbap, fried rice |
| Price | Higher | Lower |

Both are japonica rice and both work for Japanese and Korean cooking. Most Japanese restaurants in the US cook a premium short-grain or a high-quality medium-grain; most Korean home cooks in the US eat Calrose-type medium grain every day. The difference is texture, not correctness.

---

## How to Read the Bag

- **"Sushi rice"** on a label is marketing, not a variety. It just means japonica rice. Check the grain.
- **New crop** rice (*shinmai*) is moister and needs slightly less water.
- **Milling date** matters more than the expiry date. Fresher is better; buy bags you will finish in a couple of months.
- **"Calrose"** is a California medium-grain variety — the everyday standard in many Korean and Japanese-American kitchens.

## Three Rules for Cooking It

1. **Rinse until the water runs nearly clear.** Surface starch makes rice gluey rather than sticky.
2. **Rest it.** Let washed rice drain or soak 20–30 minutes before cooking, and rest it 10 minutes after.
3. **Do not stir it while it cooks.** Lid on, heat steady, hands off.

Once you have good rice, use it: [onigiri](/journal/onigiri-types-fillings-guide-japanese-rice-balls), [tamago kake gohan](/journal/tamago-kake-gohan-recipe-japanese-raw-egg-rice), [gyudon](/journal/gyudon-recipe-japanese-beef-rice-bowl) or [kimbap](/journal/korean-kimbap-guide).
