Bibimbap means "mixed rice." The mixing is not optional — it's the final cooking step. The gochujang sauce only coats everything evenly when you mix aggressively for 30 seconds before eating.

The dish is an exercise in mise en place. Each vegetable is cooked separately because they have different textures, different optimal cooking times, and different seasoning. Cooking them together produces a stew. Cooking them apart produces bibimbap.

## The Architecture

A proper bibimbap has four elements:

1. **Rice** — hot short-grain white rice, seasoned lightly with salt and a few drops of sesame oil
2. **Namul** — 4-6 individually prepared vegetable side dishes
3. **Protein** — bulgogi beef, ground beef, or a fried or raw egg for a vegetarian version
4. **Sauce** — yangnyeom gochujang, the seasoned chili paste that ties it all together

## The Gochujang Sauce

Make this first and set aside.

- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or water
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

Mix until smooth. Taste — it should be spicy, slightly sweet, and aromatic. This is the soul of bibimbap. Adjust sugar vs. vinegar to your taste.

## The Namul (Vegetable Preparations)

Prepare each vegetable separately. Season each lightly with salt, sesame oil, and garlic.

**Spinach namul:**
Blanch 2 cups fresh spinach in boiling water, 30 seconds. Drain immediately. Rinse under cold water. Squeeze dry. Season: 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 clove garlic (minced), pinch salt. Mix with hands.

**Bean sprout namul (sukju):**
Blanch 2 cups mung bean sprouts, 2 minutes. Drain. Squeeze dry. Season: 1 tsp sesame oil, salt, pinch of garlic.

**Carrot namul:**
Julienne 2 medium carrots. Stir-fry in a lightly oiled pan over medium-high heat, 2-3 minutes. Season with salt. Should be cooked but still have some bite.

**Zucchini namul:**
Cut 1 zucchini into matchsticks. Sprinkle with salt. Let sit 5 minutes. Squeeze out moisture. Stir-fry in a lightly oiled pan, 2-3 minutes, with a clove of garlic. Season with sesame oil.

**Shiitake mushrooms:**
Rehydrate 6 dried shiitake. Slice. Stir-fry with a bit of soy sauce, sugar, and sesame oil, 3-4 minutes.

## The Protein

**Quick bulgogi** (ground beef version for weeknights):
- 300g ground beef
- 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp sugar, 3 cloves garlic (minced), ½ pear or apple (grated — the enzymes tenderize)

Mix and stir-fry over high heat 5-7 minutes until cooked through and lightly caramelized.

For traditional bulgogi (sliced beef): marinate ribeye or sirloin in the same marinade 30 minutes minimum, then grill or stir-fry over high heat.

## The Egg

Traditionally, bibimbap is served with either a fried egg (yolk runny) on top, or a raw egg yolk (the heat of the dolsot pot cooks it). For regular bibimbap, a fried egg with a runny yolk is standard.

## Assembly

**Regular bibimbap:** Place hot rice in individual bowls. Arrange each namul in a distinct section around the bowl, like a color wheel. Place protein in the center. Top with fried egg. Add 1-2 tbsp gochujang sauce.

**Dolsot bibimbap (stone pot):** Heat individual stone dolsot pots over medium heat until very hot. Add 1 tsp sesame oil to coat. Add a portion of rice. Press slightly. Arrange toppings as above. Let the rice sizzle and form a crust on the bottom, 4-5 minutes. Crack a raw egg yolk on top — the heat of the pot cooks it.

## Mixing

This is mandatory and non-negotiable: before eating, mix everything together aggressively with your spoon. The gochujang sauce needs to coat every grain of rice and every vegetable. The egg yolk breaks and coats everything. Mix for a full 30 seconds.

The dolsot version creates nurungji — the scorched, crispy rice layer at the bottom. The mixing scrapes it up and distributes the crunch throughout.

---

Bibimbap is the ultimate template meal. Once you have the gochujang sauce and the technique, any combination of vegetables works. The dish scales from a quick weeknight bowl to a feast spread with eight banchan-style namul. The architecture is always the same: rice + individually prepared components + sauce + thorough mixing.
