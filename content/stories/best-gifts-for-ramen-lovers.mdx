Most ramen lovers already make ramen at home, usually from a packet. The best gifts don't replace that habit; they upgrade it. A bowl big enough to hold the broth, better noodles, a few toppings that turn a packet into a meal, and, for the serious ones, the tools to make broth and noodles from scratch.

Everything below is something a ramen fan will use within a week.

---

## Under $25: Upgrade the Bowl

### Premium instant ramen

→ [Shop premium Japanese instant ramen on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=premium+japanese+instant+ramen+variety+pack&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

There's a big gap between bargain packets and premium instant ramen. The better ones use non-fried or thicker noodles and liquid soup bases instead of powder. A mixed box of Japanese and Korean brands is the easiest ramen gift there is. For the Korean side, our [Shin vs Buldak vs Jin guide](/journal/best-korean-instant-ramyeon-shin-vs-buldak-vs-jin) covers which packets are worth it.

### Chili oil

→ [Shop Japanese la-yu chili oil on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=s%26b+chili+oil+with+crunchy+garlic&tag=borderlesskitchen-20) · [Shop chili crisp on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=chili+crisp&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

A spoonful on top changes a bowl completely. S&B's "Crunchy Garlic with Chili Oil" (taberu la-yu) is the Japanese style, crunchy and garlicky; Chinese-style chili crisp is louder. Which jar to choose is in our [chili crisp guide](/journal/best-chili-crisp-brands-how-to-choose).

### Toppings: menma, nori and shichimi

→ [Shop menma (bamboo shoots) on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=menma+seasoned+bamboo+shoots&tag=borderlesskitchen-20) · [Shop roasted nori sheets on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=roasted+nori+sheets&tag=borderlesskitchen-20) · [Shop shichimi togarashi on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=shichimi+togarashi&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

The toppings that make a ramen-shop bowl: menma (seasoned bamboo shoots, traditionally lacto-fermented), a sheet of nori tucked against the rim, and shichimi togarashi, a seven-spice chili blend, at the table. Bundle the three as a stocking-sized gift. Add a recipe card for [soy-marinated eggs](/journal/mayak-gyeran-korean-soy-sauce-marinated-eggs) and it's a full topping kit.

## $25–$60: The Serving Gear

### A proper ramen bowl set

→ [Shop Japanese ramen bowl sets on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=japanese+ramen+bowl+set+with+spoon+and+chopsticks&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

A real ramen bowl holds about a litre or more: room for broth, noodles and toppings without spilling. Most home cereal bowls are too small and too shallow, which is why packet ramen at home always looks sad. Look for a set of two with renge (the flat-bottomed ramen spoon) and chopsticks. Sizes and materials are in our [ramen bowl guide](/journal/best-ramen-bowls-and-japanese-serving-equipment).

### A noodle basket (tebo)

→ [Shop ramen noodle strainer baskets on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ramen+noodle+strainer+basket+tebo&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

The deep, long-handled mesh basket ramen shops use to cook and drain one portion at a time. At home it means you can cook noodles in a pot of boiling water and shake them dry before they hit the broth, so the broth isn't watered down. Cheap, specific and something a ramen fan won't own yet.

### A ramen cookbook

→ [Shop Ivan Ramen on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ivan+ramen+ivan+orkin+cookbook&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

*Ivan Ramen* by Ivan Orkin, the New Yorker who opened a ramen shop in Tokyo, is part memoir and part full recipe for his shio ramen, from broth to noodles. It's the book for someone ready to go beyond packets.

## $60 and Up: For the Scratch Cook

### A pressure cooker for broth

→ [Shop electric pressure cookers on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=electric+pressure+cooker+6+quart&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Rich pork-bone broths like tonkotsu take 12 hours or more of hard boiling on a stove. A pressure cooker gets a rich, cloudy broth in a fraction of the time, and it makes chashu pork too. If they're making ramen from scratch on weekends, this is the gift that makes it a weeknight project.

### A pasta roller for homemade noodles

→ [Shop manual pasta machines on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=marcato+atlas+150+pasta+machine&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Fresh ramen noodles are rolled and cut much like pasta. What makes them ramen is an alkaline salt (kansui) in the dough, which gives the springy bite and yellow colour. A home cook can make a substitute by baking ordinary baking soda, which turns it into the more alkaline sodium carbonate. A hand-cranked pasta roller does the rolling and cutting. Which machine to buy is in our [pasta maker guide](/journal/best-pasta-maker-manual-vs-electric-vs-stand-mixer).

## What to Skip

- **Novelty "ramen kits" with branded bowls and a few packets.** You pay for the box. Buy a good bowl and a variety pack separately.
- **Plastic or melamine bowls for hot broth.** A ceramic bowl holds heat and doesn't stain.
- **Huge "ramen gift baskets" from unknown brands.** A smaller box of noodles they already know is better than a lot of mystery packets.
