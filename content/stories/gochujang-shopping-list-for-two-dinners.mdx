A tub of gochujang can do two different jobs in the same week. Use a little in crema for crispy quesadillas, then stir a larger spoonful into a slow braise for tacos. Start with the size you’ll use and check the heat label before buying.

## Check the pantry first

- **For both meals** — gochujang and sesame oil. Start the crema at the low end of the recipe’s range and taste it before adding more.
- **For quesadilla night** — flour tortillas, kimchi, Oaxacan cheese or low-moisture mozzarella, and sour cream or Mexican crema.
- **For taco night** — corn tortillas, short ribs, soy sauce, aromatics and the ingredients for pickled daikon. Check the recipe quantities before shopping.
- **For what’s left** — follow the package storage directions and use another spoonful in gochujang pasta.

Go through the recipes before shopping for the full quantities, garnish and pantry staples. If someone has a food allergy or sensitivity, check the labels on sauces, pastes and prepared kimchi.
