Mexican home cooking depends on a handful of tools and ingredients that most kitchens outside Mexico don't have. Without them you get close. With them you get it right: a tortilla press, something to grind salsa in, a flat pan that can take real heat, and chiles that weren't sitting in a supermarket bag for a year.

Each item below solves one of those problems. None of it is decorative.

---

## Under $20: The Pantry

### Dried chiles: guajillo and ancho

→ [Shop dried guajillo chiles on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dried+guajillo+chiles&tag=borderlesskitchen-20) · [Shop dried ancho chiles on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dried+ancho+chiles&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Guajillo is bright and tangy; ancho is dried poblano, deep and raisin-sweet. Between them they cover most red sauces, adobos and the base of an [enchilada sauce](/journal/enchiladas-mexican-guide-recipe-red-green-mole-sauce). Good dried chiles are pliable, not brittle. A bag that crumbles has lost most of its flavour.

### Masa harina

→ [Shop masa harina on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=masa+harina+for+tortillas&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Nixtamalized corn flour: just add water to make tortilla dough. It's the difference between a homemade tortilla and a store-bought one, and it's cheap. Pair it with the tortilla press below for a complete gift. Why nixtamalization matters is in [Corn, Two Passports](/journal/corn-two-passports-korean-oksusu-and-mexican-masa).

### Mexican oregano

→ [Shop Mexican oregano on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=mexican+oregano+dried&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

A different plant from Mediterranean oregano, more citrusy and grassy. It's what pozole, menudo and many salsas are supposed to taste of. Most home cooks are using the wrong one without knowing it.

## $20–$60: The Tools

### Cast-iron tortilla press

→ [Shop cast-iron tortilla presses on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=cast+iron+tortilla+press+8+inch&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

The single tool that changes Mexican cooking at home most. An 8-inch cast-iron press makes even, thin tortillas in seconds. Choose cast iron over aluminium: the weight does the work, and aluminium presses can bend at the hinge. Line it with a cut-open zip bag so the dough doesn't stick.

### Comal

→ [Shop comals on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=cast+iron+comal+griddle&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

A flat, rimless griddle for tortillas, toasting chiles and charring tomatoes and onions for salsa. A cast-iron comal takes the dry, high heat that ruins nonstick pans. If they already own a cast-iron skillet it works, but a comal's low edge makes flipping tortillas far easier.

### Tortilla warmer

→ [Shop tortilla warmers on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=tortilla+warmer+insulated&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

An insulated pouch or lidded basket that keeps tortillas soft while the rest are cooking. It's a small gift, but it's why tortillas at a taquería are still pliable when they reach you.

## $40 and Up: The Heirlooms

### Molcajete (volcanic stone mortar)

→ [Shop basalt molcajetes on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=molcajete+volcanic+stone+basalt&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

The three-legged volcanic stone mortar used for salsa and guacamole. Grinding instead of blending gives a salsa body and a texture a blender can't. Buy real basalt: the rough, porous surface is what does the grinding. Before first use it has to be seasoned by grinding rice to a powder several times until no grit remains, so tell the recipient. Avoid the cheap smooth concrete look-alikes. The Korean counterpart is in our piece on [Korean–Mexican flavour pairings](/journal/korean-mexican-fusion-recipes-five-flavor-pairings).

### Clay cazuela

→ [Shop Mexican clay cazuelas on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=mexican+clay+cazuela+pot+lead+free&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

A glazed clay pot for moles, beans and braises, and the vessel [barbacoa](/journal/barbacoa-mexican-slow-cooked-beef-guide-recipe)-style slow cooking was built around. Buy only pieces explicitly labelled **lead-free**, because traditional glazes can contain lead.

## What to Skip

- **Taco-night kits with seasoning packets.** The seasoning is mostly salt and cumin. A bag of real chiles teaches more.
- **Aluminium tortilla presses.** Light, they bend, and they press unevenly.
- **Molcajetes that are suspiciously smooth or light.** Real basalt is heavy and rough. Smooth means concrete or resin.

## The Short Version
