**Short answer:** for most home cooks, any of the big Korean brands (Chung Jung One, Haechandle or Sempio) in a **medium-heat tub** is the right buy. The choice that matters more than the brand is **paste vs sauce**: buy the thick paste in a tub, not the thinner squeeze-bottle "gochujang sauce", unless you only want a ready-made dip. If you avoid gluten, read the label, because many gochujang pastes contain wheat.

---

## Paste vs "Gochujang Sauce"

| | Gochujang paste (tub) | Gochujang sauce (squeeze bottle) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The fermented paste itself | Gochujang thinned and sweetened, often with vinegar, garlic and sugar |
| Use | Stews, marinades, tteokbokki, any recipe that says "gochujang" | Drizzling, dipping, bibimbap topping |
| Flexibility | High; you season it yourself | Low; it's already seasoned |

Recipes that call for gochujang mean the paste. Using the sauce instead makes a dish sweeter and thinner.

---

## How to Read the Tub

- **Heat level.** Many Korean tubs print a heat scale, from mild to very hot. Medium is the safe default. Mild suits children and glazes; very hot is for people who already cook with a lot of chili.
- **Grain base.** Traditional gochujang uses glutinous rice (*chapssal*). Many mass-market tubs also contain wheat. Brown-rice versions exist too.
- **Gluten.** If you need gluten-free, look for a gluten-free label and check the ingredient list. Don't assume.
- **Storage.** Once opened, keep it in the fridge. A tub lasts for months.

## The Widely Available Brands

### Chung Jung One

The brand of Korean food company Daesang. It's widely sold in the US, including tubs labelled with Sunchang, the county in Korea long famous for gochujang. A good everyday choice with a clear heat label.

→ [Shop Chung Jung One gochujang on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=chung+jung+one+gochujang&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

### Haechandle

CJ CheilJedang's gochujang brand, one of the best-selling in Korea. Similar uses and heat range. Easy to find in Korean grocery stores.

→ [Shop Haechandle gochujang on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=haechandle+gochujang&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

### Sempio

Best known in the US for soy sauce, Sempio also makes gochujang and doenjang. A reasonable choice if you want one brand across your Korean pantry.

→ [Shop Sempio gochujang on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=sempio+gochujang&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

### Gluten-free options

Several brands sell gochujang labelled gluten-free, often made with rice only. Check the label on the exact tub you buy.

→ [Shop gluten-free gochujang on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=gluten+free+gochujang&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

## Which Size to Buy

A 500 g (about 1 lb) tub is right for most households: enough for months of stews and marinades without going stale. Buy the larger 1 kg tubs only if you cook Korean food several times a week or make your own kimchi-based dishes in bulk.
