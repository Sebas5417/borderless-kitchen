The best small gift for a cook is something they'd use up and buy again, or a tool so useful they'll wonder how they managed without it. Every item below is under $25 at typical prices (prices change; check before you buy). Nothing here is decorative.

---

## Pantry: Things They'll Use Up

### Chili crisp

→ [Shop chili crisp on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=chili+crisp+jar&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Crunchy, oily, savory chili condiment that goes on eggs, noodles, rice, dumplings and pizza. The most-requested refill in many kitchens. Our [chili oil and chili crisp guide](/journal/chili-oil-chili-crisp-guide-chinese-la-you) explains the styles.

### Furikake

→ [Shop furikake on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=furikake+rice+seasoning&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Japanese rice seasoning: sesame, nori, salt and often bonito. Rice is only the start; see [how to use furikake beyond rice](/journal/furikake-what-it-is-and-how-to-use-it-beyond-rice).

### Shichimi togarashi

→ [Shop shichimi togarashi on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=shichimi+togarashi&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Japanese seven-spice: chili, sansho, citrus peel, sesame and more. A shake on udon, yakitori or roasted vegetables. More in our [shichimi guide](/journal/what-is-shichimi-togarashi-japanese-seven-spice).

### Gochugaru

→ [Shop gochugaru on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=gochugaru+korean+red+pepper+flakes+coarse&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Korean red pepper flakes. Bright, fruity, moderately hot, and impossible to substitute. The gift for anyone who has started making kimchi. See the [gochugaru guide](/journal/gochugaru-korean-red-pepper-flakes-guide).

### Yuzu kosho or bottled yuzu juice

→ [Shop yuzu kosho on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=yuzu+kosho&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Yuzu kosho is a salty paste of chili and yuzu peel: a tiny jar lasts months. Bottled yuzu juice is the easier option for dressings and cocktails. See [yuzu](/journal/yuzu-citrus-what-it-is-and-how-to-use-it).

### Ponzu

→ [Shop ponzu on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=ponzu+sauce+citrus+soy&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Citrus-soy sauce for dipping, dressing and finishing grilled fish or steak. Our [ponzu guide](/journal/ponzu-sauce-what-it-is-and-how-to-use-it-in-western-cooking) has ideas beyond Japanese food.

### Dried guajillo and ancho chiles

→ [Shop dried guajillo chiles on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dried+guajillo+chiles&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

The base of most Mexican red sauces. Good dried chiles are pliable, not brittle. Pair with a bag of masa harina for a Mexican-pantry stocking.

## Tools: Small, Cheap, Used Weekly

### Fish spatula

→ [Shop fish spatulas on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=fish+spatula+stainless+steel&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Thin, flexible and slotted. Despite the name it's the best everyday spatula for eggs, pancakes, fish and anything delicate. Cooks who own one use it daily.

### Cooking chopsticks (saibashi)

→ [Shop saibashi cooking chopsticks on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=saibashi+long+cooking+chopsticks&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Long wooden chopsticks for turning tempura, stirring noodles and plating. Cheap, light and surprisingly precise.

### Microplane-style rasp grater

→ [Shop rasp graters on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=rasp+zester+grater&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

For citrus zest, garlic, ginger and hard cheese. One of the most-used tools in any kitchen, and the one people don't buy for themselves.

### Kimbap or sushi rolling mat

→ [Shop bamboo rolling mats on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=bamboo+sushi+kimbap+rolling+mat&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Single-purpose and necessary for [kimbap](/journal/korean-kimbap-guide). Pair with a pack of roasted nori sheets.

### Instant-read thermometer

→ [Shop instant-read thermometers on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=instant+read+meat+thermometer&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Takes the guessing out of roasting, frying oil and bread. Basic digital models are well under $25 and accurate enough for home cooking.

### Bench scraper

→ [Shop bench scrapers on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=stainless+steel+bench+scraper&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

Moves chopped vegetables to the pan, cleans the counter, portions dough. Once you have one, you use it constantly.

### Small suribachi (Japanese grinding bowl)

→ [Shop suribachi on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=japanese+suribachi+surikogi&tag=borderlesskitchen-20)

A small ridged ceramic bowl with a wooden pestle (the smaller sets fit the budget; large ones cost more) for grinding toasted sesame seeds. The difference between bought sesame paste and fresh-ground is obvious. More in [molcajete vs mortar and pestle](/journal/molcajete-vs-mortar-and-pestle).
