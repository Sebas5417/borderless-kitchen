One skillet takes care of the quesadillas. Make the spinach first: it tastes good cool, so you can give the tortillas your full attention. [Get the two-page recipe card (PDF)](/downloads/kitchen-samples/korean-cooking-fusion.pdf) for quantities and both recipes.

## What to put on the counter

For two quesadillas, set out two large flour tortillas, a cup of chopped kimchi and a cup and a half of shredded pepper jack. The dipping sauce uses sour cream, doenjang, sesame oil, rice wine vinegar and a little grated garlic. The spinach makes four side portions, so halve that recipe if you only want enough for tonight.

**If your kimchi is very juicy:** squeeze it over a bowl, then chop it. The pieces should be easy to scatter in a single layer. Large, wet pieces can slide out when you flip the tortilla.

## A dinner order that keeps the tortillas crisp

1. Blanch the spinach in salted water for 45 seconds. Rinse until cool, squeeze it dry and cut it into short lengths. Toss with garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar and toasted sesame seeds.
2. Whisk the doenjang into the sour cream before adding the vinegar and garlic. Taste a dab with a piece of kimchi before adding any more seasoning.
3. Brush a skillet lightly with sesame oil. Set a tortilla in the pan, put cheese on one half, scatter the kimchi over it and finish with more cheese. Fold. Cook over medium heat until golden underneath, then flip and melt the cheese on the second side.
4. Cut into wedges just before serving. Put the crema in a bowl for dipping so the crust stays crisp. Cook the second tortilla while the first reaches the table.
