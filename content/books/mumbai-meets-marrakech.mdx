Mumbai Meets Marrakech is a book about two pantries that have been speaking the same language for centuries without knowing it.

Cumin, coriander, turmeric, saffron — the spice routes that connected the Indian subcontinent to North Africa left fingerprints in both kitchens. This volume traces those fingerprints: ghee meeting argan oil, chaat masala meeting ras el hanout, the slow-cooked richness of a Maharashtrian curry discovering it already knows a Moroccan tagine.

Two cities. Two different oceans. One conversation that was already happening before anyone wrote it down.
