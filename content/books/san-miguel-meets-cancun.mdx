San Miguel Meets Cancún is a book about two Mexicos that share a language and very little else.

San Miguel de Allende cooks slowly, with Dutch ovens and patience and colonial kitchens that have been feeding the same families for three generations. Cancún cooks fast, with fire and citrus and the urgency of a fishing boat that needs to be unloaded before the tide changes.

This volume holds thirty recipes that live between those two modes. Some are ancient techniques given new urgency. Some are street-fast preparations that deserve a longer table. All of them answer the same question: what happens when restraint and fire decide to share a counter?
