The Reveal

Mirin.

Sweet Japanese rice wine — the reason a good glaze lacquers instead of scorching. Look for 'hon mirin', not the salted stuff.

→borderless kitchen
Why It Works

Its sugars set at a lower heat.

Mirin's glucose lacquers into gloss before refined sugar would burn — a glassy finish without the bitter edge.

The Method

Reduce, then brush late.

Simmer mirin with soy until it coats a spoon. Brush it on only in the final minutes so it glazes instead of blackening.

→borderless kitchen
The Ratio

Start one to one.

Equal mirin and soy is the classic base; a splash of sake adds depth. Reduce until glossy and it clings to the back of a spoon.
