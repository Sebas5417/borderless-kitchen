## Full description

Every Japanese–Italian substitution in Tokyo Meets Tuscany, mapped by function — not by taste, not by vibe, by what each ingredient actually does.

**What's inside (2-page PDF, print-ready):**

**Page 1 — The Matrix.** Sixteen pairings across eight categories: umami, fat & cure, finishing salt, starch & crumb, acid & alcohol, citrus & herb, heat, and body. Each row shows the Italian ingredient, what it's functionally doing in the dish, its Japanese equivalent, and a practical note on how the substitution actually behaves — sweetness, salt strength, moisture, heat character.

**Page 2 — Build Your Own Pairing.** The exact five-step method used to build every fusion recipe in the book: pick a dish you know, name each ingredient's structural role, cross the matrix, cook it as written and adjust, then name the principle you used — not just the recipe. Followed by a blank worksheet grid so you can run the method on your own dishes.

This isn't a recipe. It's the logic underneath thirty-plus of them — the reference you'll actually keep taped inside a cabinet door.

Free with email signup at borderlesskitchenseries.com/free is the single-page matrix only. This deluxe kit adds the method and the worksheet — the part that turns a chart into a skill.

From *Tokyo Meets Tuscany* — Borderless Kitchen, Vol. I.
