The Reveal

Sake.

Not for the glass — for the pan. A cooking splash, not a fine bottle. Cheap dry sake is perfect.

Why It Works

Alcohol lifts what water cannot.

It dissolves aromatic compounds locked in the fond, adds its own amino acids, then flashes off in seconds — leaving depth, not booze.

The Method

Splash, scrape, reduce.

Right after searing, off the heat, add a splash of sake. Scrape the browned bits loose. Return to heat and let it cook down.

→borderless kitchen
Sake vs Wine

Brighter. Cleaner. No clash.

Wine brings tannin and sharp acidity that can fight a delicate dish. Sake reads closer to the food's own savouriness.
