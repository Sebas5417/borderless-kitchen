You've been using gochujang like a hot sauce. Stop.

It's not built to sit on top of food. It's built to be cooked into it.

The Reveal

Fermented chili paste, not simple chili paste.

Red pepper, glutinous rice, fermented soybean powder, salt. The fermentation is what separates it from plain heat.

→borderless kitchen
What Makes It Different

Sweetness from rice. Umami from fermented soy.

Plain chili flakes give heat and nothing else. Gochujang gives heat plus a whole flavour architecture underneath it.

The Method

Bloom it in fat, the way you would tomato paste.

A tablespoon sautéed in olive oil before anything else goes in the pan — this builds heat into the fat itself.

The Comparison

It behaves like 'nduja, not sriracha.

Building the base of a sauce, not finishing it. That mental shift changes how you cook with it entirely.
