Kitchen Mistake

Cooking with toasted sesame oil destroys the thing you paid for.

Not a small loss. A total one. Here's why that bottle keeps disappointing you.

Swipe →borderless kitchen
The Reveal

It's a finishing oil. Never a cooking oil.

Toasted sesame oil is pressed from roasted seeds — the aroma lives in fragile volatile compounds.

→borderless kitchen
What Heat Does

One minute in a hot pan and it's gone.

By the time toasted sesame oil hits 180°C, the compounds that made it worth buying have broken down. You're left with plain fat.

The Rule

Add it in the final 10 seconds.

Off the heat, or straight into a cold dressing. A single teaspoon changes an entire dish when it's still intact.

→borderless kitchen
Buying Tip

Pale gold means untoasted. Skip it.

The bottle should be dark amber to brown. Check the ingredient list — cheap versions cut it with canola or soybean oil.

Your Move

Finish your next stir-fry with it. Not during — after.
